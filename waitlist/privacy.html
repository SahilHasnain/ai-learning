Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

1. Overview

TinyLearn ("we", "our", "us") is an AI learning app that helps you understand any topic in simple language through a simple loop — ask, understand, quiz, and remember. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Your learning content

When you ask a question, we send the question text and an anonymous identifier to our AI service to generate an explanation and a quiz. The explanation, quiz, and your results are stored only on your device as your learning history.

Anonymous identifier

The app creates a random, anonymous identifier that is stored on your device. It is used to route AI requests and is not linked to your name, email, or other identity. There is no account and no password.

Waitlist

If you join our waitlist, we store your email address so we can notify you when TinyLearn launches.

3. How we use your information

  • To generate clear explanations and quizzes for the topics you ask about.
  • To keep your learning history available on your device so you can reopen sessions.
  • To send you one email when TinyLearn launches, if you joined the waitlist.
  • To keep the service secure and to prevent abuse or fraudulent activity.

4. How we share your information

  • Your question text and anonymous identifier are sent to our AI service providers (hosted on Cloudflare, using AI providers such as Google Gemini or Groq) so they can generate your explanation and quiz. Providers do not retain your question content beyond what is needed to respond.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties, and we do not show ads in the app.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of TinyLearn, our users, or others.

5. How we store and protect your information

Your learning history is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. Communication with our AI service uses HTTPS encryption in transit. We do not store your prompts or learning content in logs, and provider API keys are kept as server-side secrets.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete a learning session at any time from your history — this removes it from your device.
  • Clear all app data through your device settings.
  • You may request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us.

7. Children's privacy

TinyLearn is intended for users who are at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or your personal data, contact us at: support@tinylearn.app
