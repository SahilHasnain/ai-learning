I think you're moving in the right direction, **with one modification.**

**Don't build a generic AI education app.**

Build a **generic AI learning app**.

Difference:

* ❌ "Education" = Too broad.
* ✅ "Learning" = Clear problem.

### Bad positioning

> Learn anything with AI.

### Better positioning

> Understand any topic in simple language.

### MVP

* Ask any question.
* Explain at 3 difficulty levels.
* Examples.
* Quiz me.
* Save learning history.

That's it.

### Why?

* Covers every subject.
* No syllabus maintenance.
* No content team.
* Easy for a solo developer.
* AI does most of the work.

**One warning:** Your differentiation cannot be "ChatGPT in an app." You need one core workflow that ChatGPT doesn't provide out of the box.

For example:

> **Ask → Understand → Quiz → Remember**

That is a product. ChatGPT alone is not.
